        Sound familiar?

        Busy plumbing businesses lose time between jobs, not on them.

        • The phone rings while you’re under a house

          Calls get missed or written on whatever’s nearby, and the details are gone by the time you’re back in the van.

        • Quotes get written at night

          By the time the quote reaches the customer, they may have already called someone else.

        • Parts get ordered from memory

          One missing fitting means another trip to the supplier and another visit to the customer.

        • Invoices go out days later

          Getting paid waits on paperwork, and chasing payments eats into your weekend.

        BlinQ connects each step, so the details you enter once follow the job all the way to payment.

        Questions

        Common questions from plumbing businesses.

        What is plumbing business software?

        It helps you run the business side of your plumbing work in one place: taking enquiries, booking appointments, quoting, ordering parts and getting paid. BlinQ connects those steps, so the details you enter once follow the job.

        Can my plumbers quote on site?

        Yes. With the BlinQ app on a phone or tablet, your team can capture the details, apply your pricing rules and present a professional quote while they’re still with the customer.

        Does BlinQ work with my accounting software?

        BlinQ works with Xero, QuickBooks and MYOB, and customers can pay from your quotes through Stripe or PayPal. See all integrations.

        Is BlinQ right for a small plumbing business?

        Plans start with on-site quoting for owner-operators and grow with your team. Compare the plans on our pricing page, or book a demo and we’ll help you choose.

        Is there a lock-in contract?

        No. Plans are billed monthly with no lock-in contracts, and you can change your plan as your business grows.
