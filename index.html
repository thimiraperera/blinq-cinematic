A few things to know.

Who is BlinQ built for?

BlinQ is a business management platform built specifically for blinds, curtains, shutters and awnings businesses, including retailers, manufacturers, wholesalers and dealers.

Can I create quotes on site?

Capture measurements, select products and apply your pricing rules on site. Present a professional quotation while you are still with the customer.

How can BlinQ support a growing team?

Keep enquiries, jobs and operational information connected so owners can spend less time chasing updates. A personalised demonstration shows how the platform fits sales, office, installation and production teams.

Where can I compare plans?

Compare features and monthly packages in our plans section. Complete your signup on the BlinQ pricing page.

How do I get started?

Book a personalised demonstration to see how BlinQ fits your existing processes. Smaller retailers and mobile salespeople can explore plans, with direct signup for the standalone Mobile App.
