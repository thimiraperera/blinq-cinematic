A few things to know.

Who is BlinQ built for?

BlinQ is designed for window furnishing retailers, manufacturers, wholesalers and dealers working with blinds, curtains, shutters and awnings.

Can I create quotes on site?

Yes. Record measurements, select products and prepare quotations during customer appointments. Discover the mobile app or ask for a demonstration of the full workflow.

Can my installers use BlinQ on site?

Keep installation appointments and job information connected on a phone or tablet. Ask our team which plan and configuration fit your installers.

Where can I compare plans?

Visit the BlinQ pricing page for current prices, inclusions and sign-up terms.
