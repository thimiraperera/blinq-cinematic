        About Ferncliff Blinds

        Ferncliff Blinds makes roller, venetian and outdoor blinds to order in Dandenong South. We supply window furnishing retailers, dealers and builders across Victoria, and our own install teams look after larger commercial jobs.

        Orders from our trade customers go straight into production, then through cutting, assembly, quality checks and dispatch, so every customer knows where their order is up to.

        Common questions

        Do you sell to homeowners?

        We mainly make blinds for retailers, dealers and builders. Send us a message and we’ll put you in touch with a retailer near you.

        How do I open a trade account?

        Send us a message with your business details and our trade team will call you within one working day.

        What are your lead times?

        Most roller blinds and venetians leave the factory within 10 working days. Outdoor blinds and awnings take around three weeks.

        Do you deliver outside Victoria?

        We deliver across Victoria every week, and to southern New South Wales and South Australia by arrangement.
