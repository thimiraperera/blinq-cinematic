        About Marlow Lane Interiors

        Marlow Lane Interiors is a family-run window furnishing business in Surry Hills. Since 2013 we’ve helped Sydney homeowners choose and fit blinds, curtains and shutters that suit how they live, from heritage terraces to new apartments.

        Every job starts with a free measure and quote, at your home or in our showroom. Our consultants bring the samples to you, our own installers fit every order, and we keep you updated from your quote to the day we install.

        Common questions

        Do you charge for a measure and quote?

        No. We measure and quote for free, at your home or in our Surry Hills showroom.

        How long does an order take?

        Most blinds and curtains are installed three to four weeks after you accept your quote. Shutters take around six weeks.

        Can I see samples before I decide?

        Yes. Our consultants bring fabric and shutter samples to your home, or you can visit the showroom Monday to Saturday.

        Can you motorise blinds I already have?

        For many roller blinds, yes. We’ll check your blinds when we measure and tell you what’s possible.
